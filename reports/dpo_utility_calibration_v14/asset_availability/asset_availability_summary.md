## Interpretation

- The first strict audit showed zero strict-existing rows mainly because prefix/future clips are not materialized in this worktree for many pairs.
- The recoverable split is the actionable result: rows with existing full GT video plus existing loser video can be used after a bounded prefix/future recovery step.
- Rollout-only remains blocked for real-energy retry because 15/15 rollout loser videos are missing from current assets.
- Synthetic/local-mask subsets have many recoverable rows, but this does not replace rollout-derived real-energy coverage.
